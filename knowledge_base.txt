====================================
STORE KNOWLEDGE BASE — POLICIES & FAQ
====================================

--- RETURN & REFUND POLICY ---
1. Customers may request a return within 7 days of receiving the product.
2. The product must be in its original condition, unused, and with all original tags/packaging intact.
3. Refunds will be processed within 3-5 business days after the returned item is received and inspected.
4. Shipping costs for returns are borne by the customer, unless the item was defective or incorrect.
5. Products in the "Beauty" category (cosmetics, skincare) are non-returnable once opened for hygiene reasons.
6. Electronics can only be returned if they are defective. A warranty claim must be filed for other issues.

--- SHIPPING POLICY ---
1. Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days for domestic (Indonesia) orders.
2. International shipping takes 10-14 business days depending on the destination country.
3. Express shipping (1-3 business days) is available for an additional fee of Rp50,000 (domestic).
4. Free shipping is available for orders above Rp500,000 within Indonesia.
5. Once an order status is "Shipped", the shipping address cannot be changed.
6. Tracking numbers are sent to the customer's email within 24 hours of shipment.

--- PAYMENT METHODS ---
1. We accept the following payment methods: Bank Transfer (BCA, Mandiri, BNI, BRI), Credit Card (Visa, MasterCard), E-Wallet (GoPay, OVO, DANA, ShopeePay), and Cash on Delivery (COD) for orders under Rp1,000,000.
2. Payment must be completed within 24 hours. Orders with "Awaiting Payment" status will be automatically cancelled after 24 hours.
3. Installment plans (0% interest for 3-6 months) are available for Credit Card payments on orders above Rp1,000,000.

--- WARRANTY ---
1. Electronics products come with a 1-year manufacturer warranty.
2. Warranty covers manufacturing defects only, not physical damage caused by the customer.
3. To claim warranty, customers must provide: Order ID, proof of purchase, and clear photos of the defect.
4. Warranty claims are processed within 7-14 business days.

--- STORE OPERATING HOURS ---
1. Customer Service is available Monday to Friday, 08:00-20:00 (WIB / GMT+7).
2. Saturday and Sunday, Customer Service operates from 09:00-17:00 (WIB / GMT+7).
3. Public holidays: Customer Service is closed, but the AI assistant is available 24/7.
4. For urgent matters outside operating hours, please create a support ticket through the AI assistant.

--- ORDER CANCELLATION POLICY ---
1. Orders can only be cancelled if the status is "Processing" or "Awaiting Payment".
2. Orders that have been "Shipped" or "Completed" cannot be cancelled. Please use the return/refund process instead.
3. Cancelled orders will be refunded within 3-5 business days.
4. Partial cancellation (cancelling individual items from a multi-item order) is not supported yet.

--- LOYALTY PROGRAM ---
1. Customers earn 1 point for every Rp10,000 spent.
2. Points can be redeemed starting at 100 points (equivalent to Rp10,000 discount).
3. Points expire after 12 months of inactivity.
4. Gold members (500+ points accumulated) receive 5% discount on all orders.
