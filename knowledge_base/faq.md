# FAQ

## Store Operating Hours

1. Customer Service is available Monday to Friday, 08:00-20:00 WIB / GMT+7.
2. Saturday and Sunday, Customer Service operates from 09:00-17:00 WIB / GMT+7.
3. Customer Service is closed on public holidays.
4. The AI assistant is available 24/7.
5. For urgent matters outside operating hours, customers can create a support ticket through the AI assistant.

## Order Cancellation

1. Orders can only be cancelled if the status is Processing or Awaiting Payment.
2. Orders that have been Shipped or Completed cannot be cancelled.
3. Partial cancellation of individual items from a multi-item order is not supported yet.

## Loyalty Program

1. Customers earn 1 point for every Rp10,000 spent.
2. Points can be redeemed starting at 100 points, equivalent to a Rp10,000 discount.
3. Points expire after 12 months of inactivity.
4. Gold members with 500 or more accumulated points receive a 5% discount on all orders.
