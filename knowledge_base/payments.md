# Payments

1. Accepted payment methods are Bank Transfer, Credit Card, E-Wallet, and Cash on Delivery.
2. Bank Transfer supports BCA, Mandiri, BNI, and BRI.
3. Credit Card supports Visa and MasterCard.
4. E-Wallet supports GoPay, OVO, DANA, and ShopeePay.
5. Cash on Delivery is available for orders under Rp1,000,000.
6. Payment must be completed within 24 hours.
7. Orders with Awaiting Payment status are automatically cancelled after 24 hours.
8. Installment plans with 0% interest for 3-6 months are available for Credit Card payments on orders above Rp1,000,000.
