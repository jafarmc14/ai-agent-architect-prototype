# Refund Policy

1. Refunds are processed within 3-5 business days after the returned item is received and inspected.
2. Cancelled orders are refunded within 3-5 business days.
3. Refund eligibility depends on the return policy and the condition of the returned item.
4. Shipping costs for returns are refunded only when the item was defective or incorrect.
5. Replacement requests for damaged or incorrect products may require human support review.
