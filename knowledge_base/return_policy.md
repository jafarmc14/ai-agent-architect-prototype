# Return Policy

1. Customers may request a return within 7 days of receiving the product.
2. The product must be in its original condition, unused, and with all original tags/packaging intact.
3. Shipping costs for returns are borne by the customer, unless the item was defective or incorrect.
4. Products in the Beauty category, including cosmetics and skincare, are non-returnable once opened for hygiene reasons.
5. Electronics can only be returned if they are defective. A warranty claim must be filed for other issues.
6. Orders that have been Shipped or Completed cannot be cancelled. Customers should use the return/refund process instead.
