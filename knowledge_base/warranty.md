# Warranty

1. Electronics products come with a 1-year manufacturer warranty.
2. Warranty covers manufacturing defects only.
3. Warranty does not cover physical damage caused by the customer.
4. To claim warranty, customers must provide the Order ID, proof of purchase, and clear photos of the defect.
5. Warranty claims are processed within 7-14 business days.
